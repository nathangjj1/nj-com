HOW TO USE THE EMAIL TEMPLATE IN OUTLOOK
===============================================

OPTION 1: Copy & Paste (RECOMMENDED)
-------------------------------------
1. Open the file "rota-email-outlook.html" in your web browser
2. Select ALL content (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A on Mac)
3. Copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C)
4. Open Outlook
5. Click "New Email"
6. In the email body, paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)
7. Add your recipients
8. Add subject line: "Christmas Rota 2026" (or your preferred subject)
9. Send!

OPTION 2: Open Directly in Outlook
-----------------------------------
1. Open Microsoft Outlook
2. Go to File > Open & Export > Open Outlook Data File
3. Browse and select "rota-email-outlook.html"
4. The email will open
5. Add recipients and subject
6. Send!

OPTION 3: Drag & Drop
---------------------
1. Open the file "rota-email-outlook.html" in your web browser
2. Create a new email in Outlook
3. Drag and select the entire content from your browser
4. Drop it into the Outlook email body
5. Add recipients and subject
6. Send!

TIPS:
- The image loads from your website, so it will appear automatically
- All the festive styling will be preserved
- Works with Outlook 2016, 2019, 2021, Office 365
- Compatible with Outlook for Mac and Windows
